Motion Playground

A little application for a short break. It runs entirely in your browser.

Your data stays with you

Drawings, document text, drafts, and preferences are not sent to a server. There are no analytics, remote fonts, or advertising scripts. LocalStorage saves drafts and preferences; clear site data to remove them. The hosting provider may keep ordinary HTTP logs according to the owner’s configuration.

Share if you like

Download buttons create PNG files on your device. Share uses your browser’s sharing options or copies the link.

Language and theme preferences stay on this device. By default, we follow your browser’s language and color scheme.
